Final public-code preparation draft.

Included:
- leaf and lesion segmentation
- 296-D handcrafted feature extraction
- two-stage feature pruning
- Full/Pruned SVM training and evaluation
- KNN and Random Forest parameter-search baselines
- leakage-free five-fold cross-validation
- McNemar statistical analysis
- top-level feature-group ablation
- dedicated w/o-LSL ablation
- segmentation evaluation
- unified CNN training for AlexNet, VGG-16, EfficientNet-B0, and MobileNetV3-Large
- CNN CPU/GPU efficiency benchmarks
- SVM efficiency benchmark
- dataset/class-mapping utilities
- relative-path split exporter
- fixed-version requirements
- README, MIT LICENSE, and CITATION.cff

Feature-group weighting is not used in the cleaned public pipeline.

Exact final manuscript-run reproduction still depends on archived experiment
artifacts such as the final selected-feature lists, actual split manifests,
saved models/checkpoints, reference masks, and final result CSV files.
